Privacy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Mostly Airtight Studio makes ENKI. We collect as little as we can: nothing from this website, and from the game only the crash and bug reports you agree to send. We never sell anything about you, and there are no ads.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing loaded from other companies. The company hosting it may keep ordinary server logs, as every web host does.

Crash reports from the game

The first time ENKI starts, it asks whether it may send crash reports. Until you say yes, it sends nothing. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy.

If you say yes, a report goes out when the game hits an error or crashes. It holds:

  • the game's version, and whether you were playing alone, hosting or joined;
  • your system and its version, CPU, memory, graphics card and driver, screen, and graphics settings;
  • the error itself, and the last lines of the game's log;
  • a random ID for the install, so repeated crashes on one PC can be told apart from crashes on many.

Before a report leaves your PC, the game removes your user name, your PC's name, folder paths that contain your name, and IP addresses. Our crash-report service is set not to store your IP address either.

Bug reports

Report a bug, in the main menu and the pause menu, sends only when you press Send: what you wrote, the same PC details as a crash report, the log's last lines, a screenshot if that box is ticked, and your save if that box is ticked (it starts unticked). With crash reports turned off, it makes a zip file on your desktop for you to post yourself instead, and sends nothing.

Where reports go

Crash and bug reports are kept by Sentry, a crash-reporting service in the United States, and deleted automatically after 30 days. Only the studio can read them. They are used for one thing: finding and fixing problems in ENKI.

Playing together

ENKI's multiplayer connects players' PCs directly, so the host can see the IP address of each player who joins. A host can ban an address from their own world; that list stays on the host's PC. The studio never sees any of this. When hosting, the game may ask your router to open the game's port.

Discord

Our Discord server is run on Discord, under Discord's own privacy policy.

Questions, or deleting a report

Write to enki@mostlyairtight.com. If you want a report you sent deleted before its 30 days are up, tell us roughly when you sent it and we'll remove it.
